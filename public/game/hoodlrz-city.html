hOodlrz CITY · RULES & FAQ

Access · open to everyone

The Block is open to everyone — tap ENTER THE BLOCK on the start screen and you're in. Connecting a wallet is optional and only needed if you want to free-mint a hidden NFT you recover inside the city.

The goal

Recover the 3 hidden secret souls sealed in towers across The Block: GENESIS #0000, PHANTOM #0001 and ASHEN #0002. Read the Oracles, get the exact wallet from an informant, find that tower's wallet sign, walk up and press E to claim it. You can then free-mint it to your wallet.

Controls

WASD/ZQSD move · Mouse look · Shift sprint · C/Ctrl crouch · Space jump · Left click fire · E interact / talk · J journal · F search tower · Esc pause. Gamepad and on-screen touch controls are supported.

Hoodz ($HOOD)

The street currency, shown as spinning blue-green capsules. Hit gangs to make them drop Hoodz, then walk over them to collect. Spend Hoodz in shops for health, drip and upgrades.

Combat & target lock

The Hood Blaster only fires when an enemy is locked: aim a gang into your crosshair and a red-magenta lock reticle appears, then fire. No lock means no projectile (so turning around never wastes shots). The Street Bat is a melee weapon and hits at close range. Every hit makes a gang drop Hoodz; downing one also pays $HOOD.

Police, SWAT & jail

Officers (marked POLICE) and elite SWAT units patrol on foot, and police cars and vans drive the streets: when one gets close, its gyrophare flashes red and blue and a siren wails. If you are holding Hoodz near them, a POLICE NEARBY warning fires. Get busted and you are locked up for 30 seconds: pay 100 Hoodz to get out now, or wait it out.

Street life

The Block is alive: citizens, oracles, gangs, plus stray dogs and cats, night workers and junkies wandering the blocks. Talk to anyone with E; some know more than they should.

Screenshots

Press P anytime to save a PNG screenshot of your view.

Gangs

Hostile hooded hOodlrz that roam The Block. They turn aggressive when you get close and will chase and hit you. On the radar they show as magenta squares.

Vaults & keys

Glowing blue-green keys are hidden around the city. Some special towers (villas, museums) hold a locked vault: bring the matching key and press E to crack it for a bonus NFT.

Oracles & informants

Oracles are the haloed figures in the plaza corners. Talk to them (E) for clues about the secret souls. Informants hidden in the crowd give you the exact wallet address that holds each soul.

Wallet & free mint

Connect a wallet with the CONNECT WALLET button (or from the found-NFT panel). When you recover a secret soul you can free-mint it. Real on-chain holders and NFT art load automatically if a contract is configured.

Search your tower

Press F and type a wallet address, short code or floor count. The matching tower is highlighted on the radar with its distance and direction, plus a light beam in the 3D world.

Radar legend

White outlines = buildings (thicker = secret/vault) · white dots = oracles · magenta = gangs · blue = cops · green = Hoodz, keys and weapons · the arrow is you.
